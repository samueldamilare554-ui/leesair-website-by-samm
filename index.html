Frequently asked questions

Is Lee's a licensed and insured HVAC company?

Absolutely! We are a fully licensed and insured plumbing and HVAC company, with technicians trained to meet the highest safety and quality standards.

Do you offer same-day or emergency service?

Yes! Our team provides same-day and 24/7 emergency HVAC and plumbing services throughout Central and Northern California.

What brands and systems can you repair or install?

Our technicians can service and install all major heating, air conditioning, and water heater brands, as well as custom systems for commercial HVAC applications.

Before we start any work, we provide a clear, upfront price that covers labor, materials, and support. You'll never be caught off guard by hidden fees.
